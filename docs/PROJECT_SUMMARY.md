# Project Summary

Complete overview of all physics simulations in this repository.

## Main Projects

### 1. Heat Equation
- **1D Heat Equation** (`heat_equation/heat_equation_1d.py`)
  - Rod heat transfer simulation
  - Hot spots and boundary conditions
  - Animated visualizations
  
- **2D Heat Equation** (`heat_equation/heat_equation_2d.py`)
  - Plate heat transfer simulation
  - Multiple hot spots
  - Heat flow visualization

### 2. Newton's Second Law
- **Newton's Second Law** (`newtons_law/newtons_second_law.py`)
  - Constant force example
  - Spring force (Hooke's Law)
  - Pendulum swing
  - Projectile with air resistance

### 3. Thermal Inertia
- **Thermal Inertia** (`thermal_inertia/thermal_inertia.py`)
  - Material property analysis
  - Heating/cooling cycles
  - Practical applications
